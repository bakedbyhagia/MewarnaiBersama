About Color Atelier

A small digital studio for coloring. Your artwork is saved in this browser first. If cloud sync is set up, it is also copied to a private space that only your browser can open.

Good to know

Zoom with the mouse wheel or pinch. Pan by holding Space and dragging, with two fingers, or with the hand button. Undo and redo with Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z. Paint Bucket fills any closed area of the illustration, so close gaps with the pencil if a fill spreads.
